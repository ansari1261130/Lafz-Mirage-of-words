Nigaah means gaze or glance. In Urdu and Hindustani
poetry, nigaah is often associated with the beloved's
eyes and can express attraction, affection, longing,
or emotional intensity.

Nazar refers to sight, gaze, or glance. In romantic
poetry, nazar can describe the emotional effect of
someone's look.

Chashm is a poetic word for eye and is commonly found
in Urdu and Persian-influenced poetic vocabulary.

Hijr represents separation from the beloved and is
associated with longing, loneliness, remembrance,
and emotional restlessness.
